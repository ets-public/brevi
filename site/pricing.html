Use them for anything, including commercial work, with no limits, no account and no telemetry. Paid extras for businesses are planned. Nothing that is free today will become paid.

Commitments

Paid plans add features. They never take away free ones.

Apache-2.0

The core stays open source

The compiler and everything released under Apache-2.0 stays under Apache-2.0. It will not be relicensed.

no paywall moves

Free features stay free

Paid plans add new features for businesses. No feature that is free today becomes paid.

no telemetry

The compiler does not phone home

Brevi sends nothing about you or your programs anywhere. Waitlist emails are used only to tell you when a plan opens.

Questions

Can my company use free Brevi in a product?

Yes. Apache-2.0 allows commercial use, changes and redistribution. Pro is for teams who want the extra platforms, signing and support, not a licence you need.

Why charge for anything?

To fund the project. A small paid tier for businesses pays for fixes, new Python support and the benchmarks that everyone gets for free.

What happens to my email?

It is stored with the plan you picked and your optional note, and used only to tell you when that plan opens. It is not shared or sold. Reply to that email to be removed.

When will the paid plans open?

There is no date yet. The waitlists decide which comes first. See the roadmap for what is being built now.
