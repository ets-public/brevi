Stdin is CSV with header `item,qty,price`. The item field may be wrapped in double quotes and may then contain commas. qty is an integer; price has at most 2 decimals. For each distinct item (items can repeat; sum their values), compute total value qty*price. Print `ITEM: VALUE` with VALUE to exactly 2 decimals, sorted by value descending, ties by item name ascending. Item names are printed without quotes.
