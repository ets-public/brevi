- Dynamic builds: a `unittest.mock` Mock or MagicMock stored on a class of the program (`D.__set__ = Mock()`, `D.__set_name__ = Mock()`, or under a plain name) now builds. The program's objects go into the mock sealed and come back as themselves (`side_effect`, `return_value`); `__set__`, `__delete__` and `__set_name__` mocks are called without the descriptor, as CPython's slots call them. Reading `call_args` and the other attributes that show what a mock was called with is still refused in such a program, a mock stored under another special name stops the program, and Python using a sealed object any other way stops it.
