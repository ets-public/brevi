- `Pattern.scanner()` on the dynamic build, with `match()` and `search()` that step through the text as CPython's do. A repeat count of 2**32 - 1 or more (`a{4294967295}`) raises OverflowError, as in CPython. On the typed build, `re.sub` replacement templates read `\n`, `\b` and the other escapes, octal escapes such as `\101` and `\0`, and `\g<1>`, and a bad group reference is an error even when nothing matches. Fixed by DeepSeek through `tools/ds_contrib.py` and reviewed.
