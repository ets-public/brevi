- **Functions have `__closure__`** in the dynamic build: `None` without free variables, otherwise a tuple of cell objects (`cell_contents`, comparison and repr as in CPython), in CPython's order. Cells are recorded only in programs that name `__closure__`, so making a closure costs nothing extra elsewhere; reading it where the cells were not recorded (a computed attribute name, or a method using zero-argument `super()`) is refused with an error, never answered `None`.
