- Dynamic build: a function's `__annotations__` now holds its evaluated annotations (as CPython 3.13 evaluates them when the `def` runs), and lambdas give `{}`. `functools.wraps`/`update_wrapper` copy every attribute CPython copies (`WRAPPER_ASSIGNMENTS` and `WRAPPER_UPDATES` are exposed), builtins carry CPython's docstrings, `classmethod`/`staticmethod` objects expose `__func__`, and `contextlib.contextmanager` keeps the wrapped function's metadata. A module's `__annotations__` is refused rather than guessed.
