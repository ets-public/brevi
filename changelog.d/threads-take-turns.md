- Dynamic builds run each `threading.Thread` on a stack of its own, and threads take turns where one waits: `threading.Event`, a `Lock` or `RLock` another thread holds, `join()` and `time.sleep()` now wait as in CPython (before, `start()` ran the whole thread at once and locks were never held). A thread that never waits prints what it printed before. CPython's `test_tee_concurrent` now matches.
- `bytes()` of an `array.array` (or another library object with a buffer) in a dynamic build gives its buffer, as CPython does: `bytes(array('h', [1, 2]))` is `b'\x01\x00\x02\x00'`, not `b'\x01\x02'`.
- `threading.Condition`, `Semaphore`, `BoundedSemaphore`, `Barrier` and `BrokenBarrierError` in dynamic builds, with CPython's wake order, messages and Barrier states (reset and abort while threads wait).
- `re` matches an `array.array` as the bytes of its buffer, and `bytearray`, `memoryview`, `int()`, `float()` and `collections.abc` take an `array.array` as CPython does: `bytearray(b'ab') == array('b', b'ab')` is True, `int(array('B', b'42'))` is 42 and `issubclass(array.array, MutableSequence)` is True. A `memoryview` of a library object is refused at run time (a copy could not write through). `deque` is a `MutableSequence`, as in CPython.
