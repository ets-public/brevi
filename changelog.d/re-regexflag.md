- **`re` flags behave like CPython's on the dynamic build.** `re.IGNORECASE` and the other flags are members of `re.RegexFlag`, an `IntFlag`. They print as `re.IGNORECASE`, combine as `re.IGNORECASE|re.DOTALL`, show unknown bits in hex, and still work as ints. `re.NOFLAG`, `re.UNICODE`/`re.U` and `re.DEBUG` are there too (`re.LOCALE` is for bytes patterns, which are still refused). A compiled pattern's repr shows its flags, as in `re.compile('p', re.IGNORECASE)`, and cuts the pattern at 200 characters. `Pattern.flags` leaves out `re.UNICODE` under `re.ASCII`. `Pattern.groupindex` is a read-only mapping and `Match.regs` gives every group's span. `re.compile(pattern, flags)` with an already-compiled pattern raises CPython's ValueError. A bytes pattern is refused instead of raising a misleading TypeError. On `test_re`, the dynamic build goes from 57 to 68 matching tests.
