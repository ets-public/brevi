- Dynamic build: `warnings.catch_warnings(record=True)` is no longer refused. The block gets CPython's own list, kept live, and each recorded warning names the program's file, line and module (with the line tracking from the warnings change). A warning at a line the build cannot place is still refused rather than recorded at a wrong line. It stays refused in a program that uses itertools and pickles or copies, because itertools iterators there do not pickle yet and their copies miss CPython 3.13's DeprecationWarning.
