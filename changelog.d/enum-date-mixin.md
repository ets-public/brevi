- Dynamic builds: an Enum may take `datetime.date` as its data type (`class Holiday(date, Enum)`, `Enum('E', ..., type=date)`); any other instance of a date subclass, and date arithmetic or `replace()` on such a member, is refused. Enum also gains `_add_alias_` and `_add_value_alias_`, CPython 3.13's checks on inheritance order and on extending an enum with members, `_missing_` errors chained to the ValueError, iteration over a class while its members are made, and members whose name a base's property or DynamicClassAttribute uses. `vars()` with no argument is refused at build time.
