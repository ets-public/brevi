- Dynamic builds: a str with lone surrogates can be encoded, printed and handed to CPython, with CPython's answers (#450, step 4). `str.encode`, `bytes(s, ...)` and `bytearray(s, ...)` take the `surrogatepass`, `surrogateescape`, `backslashreplace` and `xmlcharrefreplace` error handlers for utf-8, ascii and latin-1, and raise `UnicodeEncodeError` with CPython's message, range and `encoding`/`object`/`start`/`end`/`reason` attributes. `bytes.decode` takes `surrogateescape` and `surrogatepass`. `print` and `sys.stdout.write` raise `UnicodeEncodeError` for a surrogate stdout cannot encode (stdout uses `surrogateescape` under the C locale and `strict` under another UTF-8 locale, as python3's does), and `sys.stderr` writes one as `\udXXX`. `json.dumps(ensure_ascii=False)` and lone dict keys, `re.escape` and `re.Scanner` work with lone strs. A lone str passes to and from a library CPython runs.
- `print` writes the pieces before an argument whose `str()` raises, as CPython does.
