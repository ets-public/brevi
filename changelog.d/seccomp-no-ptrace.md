- Security: under `--sandbox`, CPython (`--allow-py`, `--allow native`) on Linux can no longer reach other processes. Whenever a hold is taken, the seccomp filter now also makes `ptrace`, `process_vm_readv`, `process_vm_writev`, `pidfd_open`, `pidfd_getfd` and `userfaultfd` fail with `PermissionError`, so held CPython cannot read or write another same-user process's memory, take its files, or use userfaultfd in a kernel exploit. Before, the filter held only `socket` and `io_uring_setup`, and only without a network grant. A kernel that refuses the filter now refuses such a program even with the network granted.
