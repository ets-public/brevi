- **`re.Pattern`, `re.Match` and `\N{NAME}` in the dynamic build**: `re.Pattern` and `re.Match` are real classes, so `isinstance(p, re.Pattern)` and `type(m) is re.Match` hold, and calling them raises CPython's TypeError. `\N{NAME}` escapes in patterns work, with CPython's error messages and positions, including computed CJK and Hangul syllable names. Only programs whose source has a `\N{` carry the name table.
