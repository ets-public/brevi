- Dynamic builds: a str can now hold a lone surrogate (U+D800..U+DFFF), as in CPython, from a literal such as `'123\ud800'` or from `chr(0xD800)`. `repr()`, `ascii()`, `%r`, `!r`, `==`, `<`, `sorted()`, `hash()` (dict and set keys), `len()`, `in`, `+`, `ord()`, `bool()` and `int()`'s ValueError handle such strs. Every other operation on one stops the program with a `NotImplementedError` that no `except` can catch, never a different answer; the next steps are in handoffs/string-model-design.md (#450). This makes `test_int`'s `test_error_message` match CPython.
- A docstring holding a lone surrogate is refused at build time in dynamic builds.
