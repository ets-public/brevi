- **The grammar check parses only programs that changed.** `tools/grammar_check.py` remembers every program that parsed, keyed by the grammar, Lark's version and the program's text, in `$BREVI_CACHE/grammar-ok`. A rerun, or a pull request reusing the daily run's cache, parses only new or changed programs: a full check took about 60 s on 4 cores and 1.5 to 2 min on GitHub, and a repeat takes under a second. `BREVI_GRAMMAR_CACHE=0` parses everything.
