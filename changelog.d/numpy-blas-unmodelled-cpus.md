- Fixed a wrong answer: on ARM64 (Linux, macOS, Windows) and on x86-64 CPUs without AVX2 and FMA, Brevi's own numpy printed other last digits than numpy for float `@`, `dot`, `matmul`, `inner`, `vdot`, `cov`, `corrcoef`, `polyfit` and `numpy.linalg` (seen in `lstsq`, #612). Those programs now run in CPython there, as they do when `OPENBLAS_CORETYPE` names other kernels than the CPU's own, and a binary moved to such a CPU raises NotImplementedError for them.
- Fixed a wrong answer: `np.linalg.norm` of a float32 array could differ from numpy in the last digit; it now follows numpy's `sqrt(x.dot(x))` (OpenBLAS sdot).
