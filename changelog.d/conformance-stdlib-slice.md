- **A second conformance number: the stdlib slice.** `tools/conformance.py --slice stdlib` runs CPython 3.13's `test_itertools`, `test_collections`, `test_re`, `test_heapq`, `test_bisect`, `test_textwrap`, `test_functools` and `test_string` the same way as the core slice. It has its own floor and known-wrong list, and the daily CI run ratchets it. First result: 22.2% (130 of 554 scored tests match; 24.6% on the forced dynamic build) and 81 wrong. Most of the wrong results are in `namedtuple`, `Counter`, `functools.total_ordering` and `re` (pattern repr, bytes patterns, `scanner`). These are the next fixes, and the number is published as measured ([report](bench/conformance/stdlib/report.md)).
