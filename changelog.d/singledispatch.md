- Dynamic build: `functools.singledispatch` and `functools.singledispatchmethod` follow CPython 3.13's algorithm: MRO dispatch, cache invalidation on `register`, a `registry` view, `dispatch()`, annotation-based `@f.register` including `int | str`, and classmethod/staticmethod. Dispatch is cached per concrete class in a hash map. With 1,000 registered classes it takes 0.08 s against CPython's 0.39 s. Forms Brevi cannot do faithfully are refused: ABC registration, string annotations, and `singledispatchmethod` with `abstractmethod`.
