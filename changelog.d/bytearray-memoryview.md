- **`bytearray` and `memoryview` in the dynamic build**: both were refused at build time and now run natively. `bytearray` has CPython's mutators, slice assignment and deletion, in-place `+=`/`*=`, and bytes' methods returning bytearray; `memoryview` covers one-dimensional views of bytes and bytearray (slicing, item and slice assignment, `release()`, `with`, `tobytes`, `tolist`, `toreadonly`, `hex`, hashing of read-only views). A view or an `re` iterator over a bytearray holds its buffer, so resizing it raises CPython's BufferError, and `re` group slices of a bytearray are read live. Still refused at build time: `memoryview.cast` and multi-dimensional views, subclasses of bytes, bytearray and memoryview, and either type in a program that also uses a CPython-run library; passing one to such a library raises NotImplementedError.
