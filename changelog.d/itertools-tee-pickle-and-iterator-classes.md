- Dynamic build: `itertools.tee` follows CPython's linked 57-value blocks, so a tee can be pickled, deep-copied and restored with `__setstate__`, with CPython's results and DeprecationWarnings (`itertools._tee_dataobject` included).
- Dynamic build: `zip`, `map`, `filter`, `enumerate`, `reversed` and the itertools iterator types are classes, as in CPython: `type(chain([])) is chain` is now True (it was False), and classes can be built on them (`__new__`, `__init__` with extra keywords, `chain.from_iterable` on a subclass). Copying or pickling an instance of such a subclass is refused rather than guessed, and non-subclassable iterator types are refused as bases with CPython's message.
- Dynamic build: `operator.is_` and `operator.is_not`; `zip()` now raises TypeError for an unknown keyword argument.
