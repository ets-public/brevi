- **More programs keep the typed build under the sharing proof.** The proof now follows a call of a local lambda into its body, passes on what a nested function captures to the nested functions that call it (`expr()` calling `eat()`, which changes the enclosing `pos`), counts a callback's change to its enclosing function's own list as that list's change only, and knows that compiled regular expressions and their matches, and standard-library classes such as `datetime.datetime.strptime` and `os.path.join`, change none of their arguments. Of the 551 corpus files the first version of the proof sent to the dynamic build, 391 are typed again (was 356). Setting the current key of a dict a loop walks keeps the typed build too.
- **A test hook for the sandboxed verify check.** `BREVI_TEST_TYPED_DIFFERS=1` adds a line to the typed build's output under `run --verify --sandbox`, so `tests/mcp_test.py` checks that a wrong typed answer is caught without depending on a real typed-build bug staying open.
