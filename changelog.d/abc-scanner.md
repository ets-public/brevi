- Dynamic build: abstract methods are now enforced. A class built on `abc.ABC` or made with `metaclass=abc.ABCMeta` gets `__abstractmethods__`, and instantiating it while an abstract method is left raises CPython's TypeError. Before, such a class could be instantiated, which was a silent wrong answer. The check follows CPython's `getattr(value, "__isabstractmethod__", False)`, so abstract properties, classmethods, partialmethods and `singledispatchmethod` over `@abstractmethod` count as well (the last was refused before). `property.__isabstractmethod__` and the attribute on a staticmethod or classmethod read as in CPython.
- Dynamic build: `re.Scanner` is supported. It follows CPython's construction: `.scanner.pattern` is None, the flags are raw, and without `re.UNICODE`, `\w`, `\d`, `\s` and IGNORECASE are ASCII-only. A phrase with its own groups, backreferences or inline flags is refused, because CPython numbers its groups differently inside the combined pattern.
