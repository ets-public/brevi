- `tools/ds_contrib.py` holds its spend cap as a hard stop. `--budget` is at most $2 a run. Each call is priced at its worst case (all prompt tokens uncached, a prompt bound from the request's size, every allowed output token, peak prices if peak could begin during the call) before it is sent, and its output allowance shrinks to fit, or the run stops. Replies are charged from the API's usage at the peak or off-peak price in force; a reply whose usage can't be read stops the run, and an attempt that may have been billed without a reply is charged its worst case. A lane gets its first run and one resume; a third run is refused, counted in a ledger on shared storage, and the run refuses to start when that ledger can't be read or written. `tests/ds_contrib_test.py` checks all of it against a fake API.
