- Dynamic build: PEP 479 is enforced for generators. A StopIteration that escapes a generator's body or a generator expression (raised, from `next()`, or thrown in) now raises `RuntimeError("generator raised StopIteration")` chained from it, as CPython does. Before, the generator ended silently, a wrong answer. Async generators already raised the RuntimeError; it now carries the original as `__cause__` and `__context__` too, so `contextlib` sees the exception it threw.
- The endless-generator rewrite (a seeded generator read only with `next()`, built typed as a class) is skipped when its loop could raise StopIteration: a call other than a few pure builtins, an attribute or item read, a class with dunder methods, or an import of the program's own modules. Those programs keep CPython's answer.
