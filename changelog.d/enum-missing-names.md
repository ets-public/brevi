- Dynamic builds: `enum.EnumType` (and `EnumMeta`) is `type()` of every Enum class; `member()` and `nonmember()` in class bodies; `verify()` with `UNIQUE`, `CONTINUOUS` and `NAMED_FLAGS`; `STRICT`, `CONFORM`, `EJECT`, `KEEP` and `FlagBoundary` as CPython's members; `show_flag_values`, `_iter_bits_lsb` and the `_is_*` helpers. A memberless `class S(metaclass=EnumMeta): pass` works as an Enum base; anything else it would do, and subclassing EnumType, is refused.
