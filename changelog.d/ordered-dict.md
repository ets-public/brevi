- Dynamic build: `collections.OrderedDict` now behaves like CPython's in the cases that gave wrong answers. Iterating an OrderedDict that changes size or keys raises "OrderedDict mutated during iteration"; `==` between two OrderedDicts also compares the order; `reversed(od)` is live; `repr` gives `Name()` when empty, `...` for a recursive one, and raises KeyError for a key whose hash changed; views are `odict_keys`/`odict_values`/`odict_items`. `update()`, the constructor and `copy()` follow CPython's argument checks and look each key up again. A subclass's `copy()` builds `type(od)()` through its own `__getitem__`/`__setitem__`, and `str`/`print` of a subclass name the subclass. `|`, `|=`, `fromkeys(value=...)`, `__reduce__` and instance `__dict__` work. Pickling an OrderedDict now gives back an OrderedDict in its order (before, a plain dict).
- Dynamic build: calling dict's own methods on an OrderedDict (`dict.pop(od, k)`, `dict.__setitem__(od, k, v)` and the like) is refused with a clear error. In CPython those calls leave the OrderedDict's order out of step with its contents, so Brevi does not copy that behavior.
