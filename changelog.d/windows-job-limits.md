- **Windows: `--limit mem`, held by the OS.** On Windows the program now starts suspended, goes into a Job Object with a committed-memory limit, and only then runs, so it is held from its first instruction; processes it starts are in the same job and count too, and anything left in the job is killed when the run ends. At the limit it stops with the usual `{"e":"limit",...}` line and exit 124, under `brevi run`, `brevi verify` and the MCP server's `run`. `cpu`, `file` and `fds` are still refused on Windows: its job CPU limit counts user-mode time only, and it has no file-size or handle limit.
