- Dynamic build Enums make their members as CPython does: a class's own `__new__` (with `object.__new__(cls)` or `int.__new__(cls, x)`), `_value_ = data_type(*args)` for int, str and float mixins, `__init__` on aliases too, and `super().__new__` in `__new__` raising CPython's TypeError. Reassigning or deleting a member, `mro` or an unknown `_sunder_` name as a member, and a mismatched `_order_` raise CPython's errors; `reversed()`, `copy.copy`/`deepcopy`, `dir()` of an enum class or member, and `format()`/`str.format` of a mixin member with its own `__str__` match CPython.
- `object.__str__(x)` calls `type(x).__repr__`, as in CPython (it gave the default object repr), and `int.__new__(cls, x)` (also float, str, tuple, bytes, frozenset) makes an instance of the subclass.
- A class built on `datetime.date`, `datetime`, `time`, `timedelta` or `timezone`, or `Enum(..., type=date)`, is refused at build time: the dynamic build's instances of such a class held no date and gave wrong answers.
