- **itertools are lazy on the dynamic build.** `chain`, `chain.from_iterable`, `accumulate`, `groupby`, `zip_longest`, `cycle`, `starmap`, `takewhile`, `dropwhile`, `pairwise`, `compress` and `filterfalse` now pull one item at a time, as CPython's do. Before, they read their whole input up front, so they hung on infinite inputs (`chain(count())`), missed items added after they were made, and ran key functions and callbacks in the wrong order. A grouper from `groupby` ends when the parent moves on. `map`, `zip`, `groupby` and `pairwise` can be re-entered from a callback, and so can a class's `__next__`. Re-entering an iterator that can't be re-entered raises `ValueError` instead of crashing. `iter()` raises CPython's `TypeError` when `__iter__` returns an object without `__next__`, and `zip(strict=True)` reports a longer later argument. Found by the new stdlib conformance slice: on `test_itertools`, coverage goes from 4.5% to 55% and wrong answers fall from 26 to 5.
