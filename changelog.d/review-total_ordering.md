- `functools.total_ordering` in the dynamic build matches CPython 3.13: the root comparison is chosen as CPython chooses it (`__lt__` over `__le__` over `__gt__` over `__ge__`, inherited ones included), the derived methods short-circuit and pass `NotImplemented` through like CPython's helpers and carry their `__qualname__` and docstring, and a class with no ordering raises `ValueError`.
