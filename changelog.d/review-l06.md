- **`OSError` and its friends now match CPython 3.13.** `OSError(errno, msg, file)` builds the subclass the errno names on each platform, `str()`/`repr()`/`args`/`vars()` of the exception and its `errno`/`strerror`/`filename`/`filename2` fields behave as CPython's C slots do, and `ImportError(name=..., path=...)`, `AttributeError(name=..., obj=...)` and `NameError(name=...)` accept their keyword fields with CPython's exact error messages (tests/lib/review_l06_oserror_errno.py).
