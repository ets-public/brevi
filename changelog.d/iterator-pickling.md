- **Pickling the program's iterators** (lists, tuples, strings, ranges, dicts, dict views, sets) now works when they are passed to `pickle`, with the same bytes CPython writes for list, tuple, str and dict iterators. Iterators whose CPython pickle form Brevi cannot reproduce (`reversed`, `map`, `zip`, generators) raise `TypeError: cannot pickle` instead of being saved as a plain list iterator. `next()` on a library value that is not an iterator raises CPython's TypeError.
