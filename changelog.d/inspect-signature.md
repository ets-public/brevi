- Dynamic builds: `inspect.signature` now builds for the program's own defs, lambdas, wrappers and bound methods, the `__init__` a dataclass writes, and `dataclasses.dataclass` itself, as `Signature.from_callable` already did. A Signature now has `parameters` (a mappingproxy over an OrderedDict of `inspect.Parameter`, the same one on every read) and `return_annotation`; a Parameter has `name`, `default`, `annotation` and `kind` (an `inspect._ParameterKind` IntEnum), and `inspect.Parameter.empty` and its five kinds are there. A dataclass's `__init__` now has CPython's `__annotations__`. Constructing a Signature or a Parameter builds with CPython's inspect as before, and everything else a Signature or Parameter does not have stops the program. Fixed: a dataclass with a field named `self` stored the instance in that field (its `__init__` now names its first parameter `__dataclass_self__`, as CPython does). Three more CPython dataclasses tests match.
