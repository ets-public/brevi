- Dynamic builds: `@dataclass` now follows CPython 3.13's dataclasses.py step by step. Fields read the class's evaluated annotations (Field.type, a real Field repr, the generated `__doc__` such as `C(x: int, y: List[int] = <factory>)`), ClassVar pseudo-fields are kept in `__dataclass_fields__`, `InitVar[T]` has its `.type` and repr, `__dataclass_params__` and `match_args=False` work, generated methods carry the class's qualified name, a recursive repr prints `...`, class dicts stay exact, and the frozen, mutable-default, unannotated-field, KW_ONLY and ClassVar checks raise CPython's errors. `asdict`/`astuple` take `dict_factory`/`tuple_factory` and keep defaultdict, OrderedDict and namedtuple types. `slots=True` makes a new class through type(), as CPython does. Programs that call `inspect.signature` (CPython's inspect cannot read a dynamic build's functions), or read a dataclass `__doc__` that only CPython can write, are refused at build time instead of failing at run time.
