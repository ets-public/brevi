- `str.upper()`, `lower()`, `title()`, `capitalize()`, `swapcase()`, `isupper()`, `islower()`, `istitle()`, `isalpha()`, `isalnum()` and `isspace()` follow CPython 3.13's Unicode 15.1 tables in both builds, generated into `src/unicode_case.rs` by `tools/gen_unicode_case.py`. Before, they used rustc's tables, which are Unicode 16 or newer, so characters added since 15.1 gave wrong answers (`"ɤ".upper()` gave U+A7CB). CPython's own rules now hold too: `title()` and `capitalize()` use the title case mapping (`"ǆ".title()` is `"ǅ"`), `title()` starts a new word after any uncased character (`"中a".title()` is `"中A"`), `lower()`, `swapcase()` and `title()` apply the final sigma rule, `isupper()` and `islower()` are False when there is a title case character, and `isspace()`, `split()` and `strip()` treat `\x1c` to `\x1f` as whitespace.
