- Dynamic build: itertools iterators (`chain`, `cycle`, `count`, `repeat`, `islice`, `accumulate`, `starmap`, `takewhile`, `dropwhile`, `compress`, `filterfalse`, `zip_longest`, `groupby` and its groups, `product`, `combinations`, `combinations_with_replacement`, `permutations`), and `map`, `filter`, `zip` and `enumerate`, now pickle, copy and deep-copy as in CPython 3.13. `__reduce__()` returns CPython's tuple, `__setstate__()` restores state, and a pickle loads back as Brevi's own iterator of the same type, including pickles CPython made (`itertools.cycle` pickles from older Pythons). Each itertools `__reduce__` and `__setstate__` issues CPython 3.13's DeprecationWarning at the line CPython names: the program's line for pickle, and `copy.py`'s line for `copy.copy` and `copy.deepcopy`. `copy.copy` and `copy.deepcopy` of a list, tuple, str, range, dict or set iterator now make an independent copy instead of returning the same iterator (a silent wrong answer before). Copying a generator raises CPython's `TypeError`, and other iterators are refused. `warnings.catch_warnings(record=True)` is no longer refused in programs that pickle or copy itertools iterators. Module functions such as `operator.pow` pickle by reference. `SystemError` is a builtin, and generators take weak references.
- Dynamic build: itertools constructors check their arguments as CPython's argument clinic does, so too many arguments, an unknown keyword or one given twice raise `TypeError` (`groupby('abc', key, 10)` and `permutations('abc', 2, 1)` returned an iterator before). `combinations` and `combinations_with_replacement` take `iterable=` and `r=` by keyword.
